# Léman

A floating strip 6 points from the screen's edges, with 14-point corners and a hairline border, in
lake blue-green colors: morning mist in the light appearance and night water in the dark one, with
teal for the accent. Widgets sit on the strip without tiles of their own, and popups take the same
colors.

The focused workspace gets a light teal tint and teal text; the others are muted.

This is the theme that came with StableBar, unchanged and under the same ID, `leman`, so edits you
made to Léman in Settings apply to it too.
